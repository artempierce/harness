---
name: weekly-review
description: Run a weekly review — look back at the week, list wins and blockers,
  pick next week's three priorities.
---

When the user asks for a weekly review:

1. Ask what got done this week, if they have not already said.
2. Ask what got in the way — blockers, interruptions, things that slipped.
3. From both answers, propose three priorities for next week. Keep them
   concrete enough to know when one is done.
4. Offer to remember the three priorities as a fact, so next week's review
   can check back on them.
